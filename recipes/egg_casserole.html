Egg Casserole

Description

This egg casserole recipe is quick, easy, and full of flavor. It's the perfect, low-maintenance breakfast to serve a hungry crowd.

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs, whisked
  • 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 6 slices cooked bacon, diced
  • 2 slices bread, cubed
  • 1/3 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons milk
  • ½ teaspoon minced garlic, or to taste (Optional)
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Step 1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Step 2. Mix eggs, cheese, bacon, bread, red bell pepper, green onion, milk, garlic, salt, and black pepper together in a bowl until well-combined; pour into the prepared baking dish.
  3. Step 3. Bake in the preheated oven until eggs are set, about 20 to 25 minutes.
  4. Step 4. Enjoy!
