Peanut Butter Bars

Description

These peanut butter bars are rich, creamy squares that taste like peanut butter cups, topped with smooth chocolate and ready to slice after a quick chill.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 2 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 1 cup butter or margarine, melted
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 ½ cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 4 tablespoons peanut butter

Steps

  1. Step 1. Mix together graham cracker crumbs, confectioners’ sugar, butter or margarine, and 1 cup peanut butter in a medium bowl until well-blended.
  2. Step 2. Press evenly into the bottom of an ungreased 9x13-inch pan.
  3. Step 3. Place chocolate chips and 4 tablespoons peanut butter in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave on high, stirring every 15 seconds, until smooth.
  4. Step 4. Spread mixture over crust.
  5. Step 5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before cutting into 12 squares.
